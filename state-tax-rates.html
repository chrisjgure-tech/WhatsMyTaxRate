What this table doesn't show

The rate range is for a single filer taking the standard deduction. Married-filing-jointly thresholds are almost always higher. Local income taxes — common in Maryland, Ohio, Indiana, Pennsylvania, and New York City — can add 1–3+ percentage points and are not included. Several states have phase-outs that raise the effective marginal rate above the top statutory bracket. Puerto Rico follows separate rules and is not listed here.
