State Income Tax Rates 2026

Nine states levy no income tax at all. The rest run flat or graduated rates that add anywhere from under 3% to over 13% on top of federal and FICA. Here's every number, sourced and explained.

What the table doesn't show

Local taxes can add more

Maryland counties add 2.25%–3.2% on top of the state rate. Indiana's 92 counties add 0.5%–3%. New York City adds roughly 3.1%–3.9%. Ohio and Pennsylvania have widespread municipal income taxes. The table shows only the state layer.

Effective rate is always lower

The "rate range" shows the top marginal rate — what you pay on the last dollar. Because graduated states tax lower income at lower rates, your actual all-in state rate will be meaningfully below the top figure. Use the calculator to see your real number.

No-tax ≠ no state tax

Washington has a capital gains excise tax on large long-term gains. New Hampshire's interest and dividends tax was repealed after 2024. Tennessee's Hall Tax on investment income ended in 2021. "No income tax" means wages are exempt — other income may differ.
