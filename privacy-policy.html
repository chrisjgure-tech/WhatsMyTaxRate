Privacy Policy

What data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Last updated: September 2026

Short version

Your income, filing status, and state stay in your browser. We never send those numbers to our analytics or advertising tags. We use privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics and Google Ads measurement. We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

whatsmytaxrate.com is a free, educational tax-rate calculator operated in the United States. You can reach us at hello@whatsmytaxrate.com or through our contact page.

2. Data you enter into the calculator

The income figures, contribution amounts, and other numbers you type into the calculator are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. They are never transmitted to our servers, analytics systems, or advertising partners.

The only calculator inputs that may be included in analytics data are your state and filing status — general, non-identifying categories that help us understand which states and scenarios are most commonly used. These are explicitly noted in our analytics implementation.

3. Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics, a privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics service. It collects aggregate data about page views and traffic — no personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no cookies.

We do not use Google Analytics. We do not build individual user profiles. We do not use tracking pixels from social networks.

4. Advertising

This site uses Google Ads measurement (via Google Tag Manager / gtag.js) to measure advertising performance. Google's tag may set cookies on your device in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Our ads.js implementation is designed so that income and contribution amounts are never passed to the Google advertising tag — only your state and filing status may be included in conversion signals.

5. Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies. Google Ads measurement may set third-party cookies subject to Google's cookie policy. Vercel Analytics does not use cookies.

6. Data retention and sharing

We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information. Aggregate analytics data is retained for up to 12 months for traffic analysis purposes.

We do not share data with third parties beyond the analytics and advertising services described above, and we do not share data for marketing purposes.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is generally no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data held by our third-party services (Google, Vercel), you may contact those services directly using the links in Section 4.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we do not sell personal information and do not collect personal data beyond what is described here, most CCPA rights are satisfied by default. For questions, contact us at hello@whatsmytaxrate.com.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hello@whatsmytaxrate.com

Or use the contact page.
