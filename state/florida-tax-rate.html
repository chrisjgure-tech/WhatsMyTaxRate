Florida Income Tax Rate (2026): What You Actually Pay

Florida levies no state income tax on wages — your state income tax rate is 0%. Florida residents still pay federal income tax and FICA, but the absence of state income tax makes Florida especially attractive to high earners, retirees, and anyone moving from states like New York or California.

Florida state income tax: $0

Florida levies no individual income tax on wages, salaries, or most other personal income. There are no state income tax brackets, no state filing requirement for individual income, and no withholding from Florida paychecks for state income tax.

Florida is one of nine states with no state income tax on wages. The Florida Constitution prohibits a personal income tax without a statewide voter referendum. Nine states have no wage income tax: Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, and Wyoming.

What Florida residents do pay in 2026

  • Federal income tax: The same progressive brackets as every other US state — 10% to 37%
  • Social Security (FICA): 6.2% on wages up to $184,500
  • Medicare (FICA): 1.45% on all wages (0.9% surcharge above $200,000 single)
  • State income tax: $0
  • State sales tax: 6% state rate; counties add up to 2.5% discretionary surtax
  • Property tax: No state property tax; county/local effective rates typically 0.8%–1.2% of assessed value — moderate by national standards

Why Florida is a top retirement destination

Florida's combination of no state income tax and no state tax on Social Security, pensions, or retirement account withdrawals makes it particularly valuable in retirement. A retiree drawing $80,000 from a 401(k) plus Social Security pays zero Florida state income tax on any of it — compared to paying thousands in California, New York, or many other states.

Florida also does not have an estate tax or inheritance tax, further favoring wealth transfer.

Federal income tax on Social Security still applies based on your combined income — Florida cannot eliminate that. Use the retirement section of the calculator to see your federal Social Security tax.

Frequently asked questions

Does Florida have a state income tax?

No. Florida has no individual state income tax on wages or most personal income. Florida residents pay $0 in state income tax.

Does Florida tax Social Security or pension income?

Florida has no state income tax at all — Social Security, pensions, 401(k)/IRA withdrawals, and other retirement income are fully exempt from Florida state income tax. This is one of the primary reasons Florida attracts retirees. Federal taxation of Social Security still applies based on your combined income.

What taxes do Florida residents pay?

Florida residents pay federal income tax and FICA (Social Security + Medicare), the same as residents of all other states. Florida also has a state sales tax of 6% plus county surtaxes. Property taxes are set locally and are typically moderate (effective rates of 0.8%–1.2% of assessed value).

Is Florida better than Texas for taxes?

Both Florida and Texas have no state income tax, so the income tax savings are identical for residents of both states. They differ on property and sales taxes: Texas tends to have higher property tax rates (1.5%–2.5%), while Florida's are moderate (0.8%–1.2%). For non-homeowners or high earners, the difference is minimal.
