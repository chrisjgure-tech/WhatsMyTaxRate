What's My Tax Rate

About this tool

What it is

What's My Tax Rate is a free, plain-language calculator for US W-2 earners. It shows your federal effective rate (what you actually pay on every dollar of income) alongside your federal marginal rate (the bracket you've landed in), plus your FICA and state liability — so you can see the full picture in one place.

The core insight: most people quote the top bracket they hit as "their" tax rate. That number is almost always significantly higher than what they actually owe, because progressive brackets, the standard deduction, and FICA phase-outs all work in your favor before the math is done.

Who built it

This tool was built by a team of independent developers and writers focused on personal finance clarity. Our goal is to replace the anxiety of "I'm in the X% bracket" with a concrete, honest number — calculated in your browser, never sent to a server.

Data sources and methodology

All rate schedules and thresholds are pulled directly from IRS and SSA publications. Calculations assume W-2 wage income and the standard deduction only.

  • IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 — 2026 federal income tax brackets, standard deduction amounts, and contribution limits
  • SSA OACT — Cost-of-Living Adjustments — 2026 Social Security wage base for FICA calculations
  • Tax Foundation — State Income Tax Rates — state bracket schedules and flat rates
  • IRS Statistics of Income — Historical Table 23 — top statutory federal marginal rate history, 1955–2026
  • IRC §933 — Puerto Rico source income exclusion for bona fide residents

What this tool does not cover

To keep results clear and actionable, the calculator excludes: local and municipal income taxes, the Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT), the Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT), capital gains and qualified dividend rates, self-employment tax, itemized deductions (except in the dedicated section), and most credits. Your actual tax liability will differ from these estimates.

This is not tax or legal advice. Consult a qualified tax professional before making financial decisions based on these estimates.
