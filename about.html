Methodology

All calculations use the 2026 tax year parameters unless otherwise noted. The calculator assumes:

  • W-2 wage income only (no self-employment, capital gains, or investment income)
  • The standard deduction — no itemized deductions unless you use the itemize tool
  • No dependents, credits, or alternative minimum tax (AMT) adjustments
  • FICA at the statutory rate: 6.2% Social Security on wages up to the SSA wage base, plus 1.45% Medicare on all wages
  • State tax applied at the effective rate for your filing status, using the standard deduction where available

The effective rate is total tax divided by gross income. The marginal rate is the rate that applies to your next dollar of ordinary income across federal, FICA, and state.

This tool is reviewed and updated every January to reflect new IRS revenue procedures and state tax law changes. The most recent update incorporated IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 (2026 brackets, standard deductions, and FICA wage base).

Data sources

  • IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 — 2026 federal tax brackets, standard deductions, and FICA wage base
  • SSA — Contribution and Benefit Base — Social Security wage base
  • Tax Foundation — State Income Tax Rates — 50-state individual income tax parameters
  • IRS SOI Table 23 — historical top marginal rates

State tax data is verified annually against official state revenue department publications. Puerto Rico rates use the 2018-reform brackets, the latest published; a 2026-specific schedule was not available at time of publication.
