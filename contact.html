When to get in touch

The most useful messages are ones where a specific number looks wrong — a state rate, a bracket threshold, or a FICA cap that seems off. Include your income, filing status, and state and we can check it against the source data quickly.

What we respond to

Rate corrections

If a state rate, deduction, or bracket threshold looks wrong, let us know which state and what you expected to see. We verify against IRS publications and Tax Foundation data and update when something is genuinely out of date.

Calculator feedback

If the tool behaves unexpectedly — a wrong result, a missing state, a layout issue on your device — a short description of what you were doing is enough for us to reproduce it.

General questions

Questions about how a specific feature works or what an output means are welcome. For personal tax advice, though, please consult a qualified CPA or enrolled agent — we're an educational tool, not a professional tax service.
